2. Comparison method

The complete author manuscript was inspected for antecedents of the revised
paper's claims. Each item below separates a concept or method already present
from a more precise specification, correction, added case or executed check in
the revision. A detail is not treated as novel merely because the revision
gives it a new symbol, entity name or equation number. Reported absences concern
the inspected author version; they are not claims about unseen changes in the
publisher's final text. Current-paper locators use subsection headings because
final page and section numbers may change during typesetting.

3. Detailed comparison

01. Standards composition and fitness for use
    Prior locus: Sections 1--2, pp. 1--4; Table 1, p. 3.
    Inherited basis: Complementary governance, evaluation, sensor-performance,
    runtime and semantic layers; intended use, requirements, metrics, methods,
    thresholds, provenance and accountability.
    Revision: Background and relationship to existing work; Formal evidence
    model. The contribution is a constrained composition with explicit field
    associations and applicability rules. Neither the multilayer motivation
    nor fitness-for-use principle is claimed as new.

02. Metric catalogue and scalar aggregation
    Prior locus: Section 3, Eq. (1), p. 4; Table 2, pp. 5--7.
    Inherited basis: Weighted sum of normalized dimensions, weights summing to
    one, and a warning that the aggregate must not replace dimension-specific
    evidence. The catalogue includes completeness, exponential freshness,
    schema-check ratios, uncertainty, calibration age and evidence completeness.
    Revision: Metrics, policy scores, and non-compensable gates. Added details
    are an instantiated policy, denominators and bounds, missing-input behavior,
    separation of uncertainty reserve from runtime quality, and executed
    synthetic decisions. The weighted sum and freshness recipe are inherited.

03. Critical gates
    Prior locus: Section 4.2, p. 8; related exclusions in Sections 4.1 and 6.
    Inherited basis: Reject, delay or escalate when calibration, security or
    evidence completeness is below a required minimum.
    Revision: From source qualification to observation applicability; Metrics,
    policy scores, and non-compensable gates. The explicit Boolean eligibility
    product and treatment of unknown evidence refine an existing gate principle.
    The synthetic fixtures exercise supplied gate inputs; they do not establish
    integration with operational calibration or security infrastructure.

04. Fusion
    Prior locus: Section 4.2, Eq. (3), p. 8.
    Inherited basis: A heuristic that multiplies source-quality scores into
    inverse-variance-like weights with an epsilon in the denominator. The
    inspected version gives no corresponding derivation or numerical test.
    Revision: Conditional uncertainty-aware fusion. The heuristic is corrected
    to binary eligibility with compatible support, unbiased-error assumptions
    and applicable positive variances. Independent and correlated cases are
    distinguished, the empty eligible set is explicit, and a numerical example
    is executable. Standard inverse-variance estimation is not a new algorithm.

05. Evidence tuple and field associations
    Prior locus: Section 1, p. 2; Table 2, p. 7; Table 3, p. 11;
    Section 6, p. 12.
    Inherited basis: Target/property, dimension, metric/value, criterion,
    method/tool/standard, time window, provenance, evaluator, evidence,
    hash/signature and result.
    Revision: Formal evidence model. The tuple formalizes existing ingredients
    and adds explicit applicability and verification-status interpretation,
    associated metric/value/unit/criterion representation, and a distinction
    between an original report and a digest of a derived representation.

06. Source profiles, validity and lifecycle
    Prior locus: Table 2, p. 6; Table 3, p. 11; conclusion, pp. 12--13.
    Inherited basis: Calibration intervals, certification status, source
    profiles, rule validity/version, label validity periods and conditional
    context rules. Validity and versioning are not newly introduced here.
    Revision: From source qualification to observation applicability. Added
    precision covers joint unit/type, configuration, scope, time and state
    eligibility; explicit unqualified, valid, suspended, expired and superseded
    states; effective-dated revocation; and preservation of evidence versions.

07. Dual NGSI-LD representation
    Prior locus: Section 6, Listing 1.1, p. 10; Table 3, p. 11;
    surrounding discussion, pp. 10--12.
    Inherited basis: Compact property sub-properties plus linked detailed
    quality assessments, including an illustrative pollutant value and score.
    Revision: The NGSI-LD application profile and reproducible listing fixture.
    The current score is calculated from declared synthetic inputs, the local
    context is complete, and linked files plus a generator are supplied.
    These are concrete implementation refinements of the inherited pattern.

08. Supporting entity families
    Prior locus: Table 3, p. 11.
    Inherited basis: DataQualityAssessment, SensorQualityProfile,
    DataQualityRule, DataQualityIncident, DataQualityLabel,
    AIOutputQualityAssessment and the quality-dimension vocabulary.
    Revision: The application-profile constraints refine these existing roles.
    Their names or existence are not presented as new journal contributions or
    as acceptance into a canonical Smart Data Models repository.

09. Processing placement and failure handling
    Prior locus: Section 5, pp. 9--10; Section 6, pp. 11--12.
    Inherited basis: Inexpensive edge/gateway checks, expensive back-end
    analysis, ingestion validation, retention of suspect records with flags,
    broker links and publication of assessments.
    Revision: High-frequency evidence generation and integrity; architecture
    and Toolbox mapping. Added engineering detail includes bounded windows,
    immediate/provisional versus final-window status, deterministic publication,
    idempotency, failure handling and evidence preservation. Only the explicitly
    scoped local processing costs are measured.

10. Evidence bundle and integrity
    Prior locus: Section 4.5, p. 9; Section 6, p. 12.
    Inherited basis: Signed labels or credentials; an export containing semantic
    representation, usage policy and quality evidence, including a method,
    window, evaluator and signature or hash.
    Revision: A concrete derivative-byte contract, computed derivative digests,
    source-unverified labels, a standalone test-key signature/tamper example,
    and stated issuer-authentication limits. Bundling and signing in principle
    are inherited. The source reports are not authenticated by these tests.

11. Specific report-summary reconstruction
    Prior locus: Section 4.1, pp. 7--8.
    Inherited basis: CEN/TS 17660 and laboratory/field performance metrics.
    The inspected author version does not contain the current 171-assertion
    corpus, its three-device tables, seven field windows or threshold-utilisation
    reconstruction.
    Revision: Source corpus and evidence boundaries; reported reconstruction
    results. Added work is the specific arithmetic and representation audit,
    including identifier collisions and group-statistic semantics. It does
    not constitute newly performed sensor certification or independently
    reproduced laboratory/field experiments.

12. Executable semantic, failure and integrity checks
    Prior locus: Sections 5--6, pp. 9--12.
    Inherited basis: JSON Schema, SHACL, semantic validation, hashes and flags
    are discussed. No corresponding all-record execution, RDF round trip,
    malformed-input suite, twelve-case synthetic decision execution or captured
    signature-tamper result is reported in the inspected version.
    Revision: Validation protocol, local results and executable supplement.
    The increment is an instantiated and reproducible evaluation of this
    profile, not invention of the validation technologies.

13. Local representation cost
    Prior locus: No corresponding workload or repeated timing result in the
    inspected author manuscript; conceptual placement appears in Sections 5--6.
    Revision: High-frequency evidence generation and integrity; local cost
    results. The revision adds a defined 3600-observation synthetic workload,
    seven recorded runs and a per-observation versus windowed representation
    comparison. Timings concern assembly, hashing and signing. They exclude
    gate evaluation, signature verification, network, broker and database costs.

14. Toolbox and Madrid design applications
    Prior locus: Sections 4 and 6, pp. 7--12.
    Inherited basis: Generic smart-city, digital-twin, industrial and data-space
    applications. No current EU LDT Toolbox block mapping or Madrid governance
    blueprint appears in the inspected author manuscript.
    Revision: The specific capability/evidence mapping and municipal governance
    blueprint are additional design applications. Availability of a replication
    testbed does not supply missing profile-specific deployment measurements.

15. Cross-domain scope
    Prior locus: Sections 4.1--4.6, pp. 7--9; Section 6, pp. 10--12.
    Inherited basis: Air quality, toxic-gas safety, crowd sensing, industrial
    AI, marketplaces, AI outputs and other Smart Data Models domains.
    Revision: More specific transfer examples and applicability conditions
    respond to the reviewer. Cross-domain applicability itself is inherited,
    and the present experiments do not validate all those domains.

4. Consequence for contribution claims

The journal revision is an incremental specification, correction and evaluation
of the antecedent profile. Defensible additions are the explicit applicability
and missing-evidence contract, conditional fusion correction, specific supplied
corpus reconstruction and representation repairs, executable checks, bounded
local cost measurements, and Toolbox evidence-propagation mapping. The evidence
ingredients, weighted score, freshness recipe, gate principle, dual pattern,
entity families, validity/version concepts, processing split and bundle are
credited to the conference baseline in the revised manuscript.

This comparison does not establish publisher-version identity, authenticity of
the source sensor reports, deployment of the profile, or publication of the
profile in an external standards repository.
